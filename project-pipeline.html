02
Approach

Building the full-funnel engine

The pipeline problem at RetinAI wasn't awareness — it was conversion architecture. Clinicians and pharma researchers were finding the product but not taking action. My diagnosis: too much cognitive load at every stage, not enough trust-building content between interest and intent, and a conference presence that wasn't converting attention into pipeline.

I rebuilt the funnel from first principles: neuromarketing-led landing page redesign, a content program structured to move prospects from awareness to intent, and a complete overhaul of conference materials. I redesigned all brochures and collateral from scratch — differentiating messaging by audience (clinicians, pharma partners, research networks), making complex science immediately legible, and creating enough visual identity to stand out in a booth-heavy environment. I also put in place dedicated events within conferences: internal presentations and KOL-led sessions that turned passive foot traffic into active conversations.

Channels

Where the leads actually came from

In conservative medical markets, the channel mix looks different. Paid search generated almost no qualified pipeline — retina specialists don't click ads for clinical AI tools. The real drivers were: conference activation, LinkedIn organic thought leadership, and SEO/AEO-optimised content that appeared when researchers were actively searching for retinal AI solutions.

05
Takeaways
1
Cognitive load is the enemy of pipeline. In regulated, technical markets, the biggest conversion killer isn't lack of awareness — it's the effort required to understand and trust the product. Every reduction in cognitive load at a key decision point translated directly to conversion rate improvement.
2
Channel fit matters more than channel volume. We spent almost zero on paid search and paid social. The pipeline came from conferences, organic content, and inbound referrals — channels where the signal-to-noise ratio matched what clinical buyers actually trust.
3
Marketing and sales alignment is the multiplier. The conversion gains weren't marketing-only. Close collaboration with the sales team on handoff moments, demo scripting, and follow-up sequences meant qualified leads actually became opportunities instead of stalling at the gate.
4
AI-native content is already driving pipeline. By 2024, a measurable portion of inbound leads self-reported finding RetinAI through AI-generated research answers — not Google search. This channel will only grow.
