The challenge

The website read like a research paper and captured almost nothing — an average of 15 conversions a month and two lead-capture forms on the entire site. The funnel had no defined stages, so leads leaked out untracked.

I treated the site as the top of a revenue system, not a brochure — rebuilding conversion paths, instrumenting every lead, and growing qualified organic traffic in parallel.

Demand generationCROWebsiteSEOHubSpot
Strategy +
  • Rebuilt conversion paths: redesigned the homepage, reworked existing product pages and launched brand-new ones — writing the messaging for each — and simplified the overall UX so key pages were reachable in fewer clicks, lifting both conversion rate and volume.
  • Added strategic forms and a newsletter capture, with custom properties (event, product interest, referral) so every lead arrived tagged and routable.
  • Ran on-page and technical SEO in parallel to grow qualified organic traffic, not just volume.
Results +
  • Marketing-sourced pipeline value grew from 233,600 CHF in 2022 — the year before I joined — to 4,560,706 CHF at the end of 2025: a 19.5× increase.
  • Product-page traffic jumped across the board — flagship pages up +2,611%, +977%, +452%.
  • New marketing-sourced leads converted to opportunities at 19% — proving the funnel captured quality, not just clicks.
