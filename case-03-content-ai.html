A two-track content system, built AI-native

There was no content calendar and no clear product story — 90% of people I spoke to couldn't say what RetinAI's products actually did. I built a content engine on two tracks, repositioned the portfolio around clarity, and ran the whole thing with AI systems I built myself.

The challenge

Content was ad hoc and the product messaging assumed the reader was already an expert. After sitting in on sales and demo calls, I found the core problem wasn't awareness — it was comprehension.

I rebuilt the content function around a calendar, a two-track strategy, and messaging that put the benefit before the technology — and I used AI not as a shortcut, but as infrastructure. Marketing an AI company with AI-native methods meant I understood the technology I was selling, because I used the same class of tools every day.

Content strategyProduct marketingAI-native productionClaude CodeSEO/AEO/GEO
Strategy +
  • Created the content calendar and a two-track model: a product track (clarity, use cases, benefits) and an event track (KOL webinars, conference amplification).
  • Built my own AI agent workflows in Claude Code (VS Code, .md-defined agents) to scale production — research, first drafts, and SEO/AEO/GEO optimisation — fed with my strategy, my existing content and my own data so the output stayed on-brand.
  • Used Paperclip (open-source) to rebuild the website — 100+ pages, rewritten three times faster by combining my content strategy with my data.
  • Shifted the social mix to 70% problem-solving, 30% authority-building; repositioned every product with before/after messaging and audience-specific identity.
Results +
  • Turned a comprehension gap (≈90% unclear on products) into a clear, benefit-led product story across brochures, site and collateral.
  • Rebuilt 100+ website pages at 3× speed — an output normally requiring a team, delivered as a team of one by using AI as leverage without losing editorial quality.
  • Grew organic inbound and positioned RetinAI as a cited authority in retinal-AI queries across search and AI answer engines.
