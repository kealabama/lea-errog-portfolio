The challenge

Content split into two dead ends: deeply educational posts ("What is telemedicine?") that talked past a professional audience who already knew the basics, and social posts that only ever celebrated us — an award, a citation, a CEO speaking slot — always announced after the fact, never planned. Nobody had flagged it; I found the pattern by going through everything we'd published, channel by channel.

The comprehension gap was the bigger surprise — it wasn't just prospects who couldn't explain our products clearly, it was people inside RetinAI too. I didn't have a shortcut for this: I built the picture through calls across every function in the company, and through running my own product demos with prospects at conferences.

Strategy +
  • Diagnosed the two-failure pattern myself — educational-only blogs missing our own audience, and reactive, ego-only social — then built the comprehension picture through cross-functional calls and my own conference demos with prospects.
  • Created the content calendar and a two-track model: a product track (clarity, use cases, benefits) and an event track (KOL webinars, conference amplification, always planned ahead of the event, not announced after).
  • Rebuilt product messaging and design from scratch — every brochure and the website's product pages — mapped to 3 distinct audiences across 6 products/solutions, benefit before technology, written and designed by me.
  • Gave the Research team's work a real amplification channel: co-created blog articles with them, and promoted their published papers across the site, social and conferences — including helping promote their posters in person.
  • In 2026, once the system already worked by hand, I built my own AI agent workflows in Claude Code (.md-defined agents) and used the open-source Paperclip framework to rebuild 100+ website pages three times faster.
Results +
  • Turned a comprehension gap that included people inside RetinAI itself into a clear, benefit-led product story — every brochure and the site's messaging rebuilt end-to-end.
  • The redesigned brochures made a visible difference in person — clearer pitches and easier selling at conferences, where the old collateral had been part of the problem.
  • Gave Research's publications a real amplification channel — blog co-creation, social promotion, and in-person poster promotion at conferences.
  • In 2026, rebuilt 100+ website pages at 3× speed with AI agents I built myself — an output that would normally need a team, once the manual system already proved what worked.
  • Grew organic inbound and positioned RetinAI as a cited authority in retinal-AI queries across search and AI answer engines.
