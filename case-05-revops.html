The challenge

The funnel had no shared definitions — marketing and sales disagreed on what a qualified lead even was, and hundreds of contacts sat untouched because no one owned them. I built the operational backbone from scratch.

This is the foundation the attribution model (01) runs on: without clean lifecycle stages and workflows, none of the revenue analytics would have been possible.

Strategy +
  • Built the HubSpot setup end-to-end myself: the 8-stage lifecycle (Subscriber → Evangelist), lead-status sub-stages, and the automated workflows routing every form submission to the right owner.
  • Partnered with our Product-Enabled Sales Manager to design an advanced, product-specific sales setup for Discovery for Clinics — automated MQL→SQL hand-overs, owner assignment, notifications, and a full activity flow from form submission to closed deal.
  • Aligned marketing and sales on one funnel, surfacing previously overlooked leads (e.g. 160 dormant Pharma subscribers ready to route to sales).
  • Ran GTM for launches: engaged KOLs for product testing and event presentations, and executed the campaigns behind 3 product launches, anchoring credibility on CE + FDA clearance.
Results +
  • Rolled out a lifecycle and workflow system that made the whole pipeline measurable — the foundation the attribution model runs on.
  • Launched 3 products, including Discovery for Clinics (CE + FDA) and the RetinAI Apps via Heidelberg's AppWay.
  • Grew qualified demo requests +180% year-on-year on the flagship launch.
