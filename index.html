I specialise in making the deeply technical immediately desirable. At the intersection of data science and human psychology, I build marketing systems that are measurably ROI-positive and anchored in neuromarketing principles that reduce friction to conversion.

This is my philosophy wherever I work. At RetinAI, a Swiss AI startup building diagnostic tools for ophthalmology, I applied it from day one — rebuilding the demand generation engine, repositioning the brand, and building a content ecosystem that earned real authority.
