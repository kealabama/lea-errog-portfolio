Turning conferences into pipeline, press — and a team

The challenge

Events were a cost centre with no engagement strategy and no follow-through. I turned them into a lead-and-earned-media engine — and as the function grew, into a team.

The proof of PR isn't a vanity metric — it's the earned media itself: journalist interviews at EURETINA, a partnership announcement presented by our CEO at OIS, KOL content that gave sales warm touchpoints across the industry.

Event marketingTeam leadershipPREarned mediaKOL engagement
Strategy +
  • Owned events end-to-end at first: budget, logistics, booth design, and an in-booth engagement strategy (KOL presentations, live polls) repurposed into digital content afterward.
  • Grew the function into a team: hired and managed an Event Specialist, then a second (reporting through the first), and stepped back into direct management of the second during the first's maternity leave — keeping the function running through the transition.
  • Ran journalist and press relations: press-release drafting and promotion (e.g. the Retina Consultants of America partnership, presented by our CEO at OIS), plus on-the-ground video interviews with industry leaders (Canon Medical's Director of Eye Care, IAPB, practising clinicians) at EURETINA.
Results +
  • Coordinated 15+ events in 2023–2024; conferences closed fastest of any channel (avg. 1.7 months to close).
  • Grew webinar registrations +54% (65 vs. 42 leads), with 86% from Pharma, on 1,000 CHF vs. the prior 1,500 CHF.
  • Generated earned media — journalist interviews and KOL content — giving sales warm re-engagement touchpoints across the industry.
