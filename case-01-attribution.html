Building marketing where none existed — and proving it drives revenue

When I joined in February 2023, there was no marketing function — no campaigns, no pipeline, no way to know what worked. I built it from zero, and instrumented it as I went: from 0% of revenue marketing-touched to 70%, in three years.

The challenge

There was no marketing function to inherit — no campaigns running, no pipeline being tracked, no way to know what worked once I started building. I had to create the activity and the way to measure it at the same time, or risk building on guesswork.

As the function grew, I built an attribution framework in HubSpot that separates sourced (marketing created the deal) from influenced (marketing touched an existing account), and tracks pipeline and closed-won on separate timelines, so every CHF I spent could be justified by what it produced.

Strategy +
  • Built the marketing function from zero — no campaigns, no pipeline, no team playbook — and designed the attribution model alongside it, not after.
  • Defined a sourced vs. influenced attribution model — an attribution metric, not an ownership one: sales owns every deal, marketing evidences its touch.
  • Separated the leading signal (pipeline share) from the lagging one (closed-won share) to explain why revenue impact compounds a year behind pipeline.
  • Built the analysis on cohort logic — deals created in a year, tracked to close — so CAC and ROI aren't distorted by deals that close on next year's budget.
Results +
  • Marketing's share of pipeline crossed 50% in 2025 — the inflection year where it became the majority driver of new business.
  • Closed-won caught up on the expected lag: 70% of H1 2026 closed-won revenue was marketing-touched — sourced plus influenced — up from 0% in February 2023.
  • Marketing-sourced leads close at a comparable win rate to pure outbound (17.5% vs. 21.9%) — proving the leads convert at the same quality, at higher volume.
  • Marketing-touched deals close 2.5 months faster, shortening the ROI window on every franc of marketing spend.
